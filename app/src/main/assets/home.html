About This App

Find any DIU faculty member in seconds. DIU Faculty Finder is a simple directory app built to help Daffodil International University students quickly find any faculty member's department, room number, email, and contact details — all in one place, without digging through multiple web pages.

Built with care for the Faculty of Science & Information Technology (FSIT), covering the CSE, SWE, ITM, MCT, and CIS departments — this app is a student project aimed at making campus life a little easier.
